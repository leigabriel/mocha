# Tiger

## Appearance
A large, muscular feline with a long body, broad chest, powerful shoulders, strong hindquarters, large paws, and a long tail. Its head is broad, with rounded ears and a short muzzle.

## Body and Surface
Short, dense fur covers the body. The base coat is typically orange to reddish-orange, with black vertical stripes and pale cream or white areas on the underside, cheeks, and around the eyes. Stripe patterns vary between individuals and should continue naturally across the torso and limbs.

## Head and Features
The face has forward-facing eyes, a broad nose, strong jaws, and long whiskers emerging from visible follicles. White facial patches and dark stripes frame the eyes and cheeks. Ears are rounded, often with pale spots on the back.

## Limbs, Paws, and Tail
The legs are muscular and end in broad paws with retractable claws. The long, flexible tail has dark rings or bands. Paw pads are thick and textured, while the claws are hard and curved.

## Modeling and Materials
Build substantial muscle volume beneath a short matte coat. Apply stripes as coherent markings that follow body form, not as disconnected surface stickers. Use fine fur detail, subtle nose and eye gloss, and distinct materials for paw pads and claws.
