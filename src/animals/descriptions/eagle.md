# Eagle

## Appearance
A large bird of prey with a powerful body, broad wings, strong shoulders, a hooked beak, and sturdy legs. The head, tail, and plumage colors vary by species and age.

## Body and Surface
Dense feathers cover the body, with larger layered feathers across the wings and tail. Plumage may be dark brown, gray, black, or white, sometimes with contrasting head or tail markings. Feather direction follows the body and overlaps in organized rows.

## Head and Features
The head has forward-facing vision relative to many birds, though the eyes remain positioned on the sides of the skull. The beak is large, sharply hooked, and designed for tearing food. Eyes are prominent and glossy; the facial feathers often form a defined brow.

## Wings, Legs, and Talons
Long, broad wings have separated primary feathers at the tips. Powerful, feathered legs end in large toes with strongly curved talons. The tail is made of long, broad feathers used for steering and balance.

## Modeling and Materials
Emphasize wing span, layered flight feathers, hooked beak, and curved talons. Keep feather surfaces mostly matte with subtle variation. Use harder, smoother materials for the beak and claws, and match the plumage pattern to a specific eagle species.
