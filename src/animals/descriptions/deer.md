# Deer

## Appearance
A slender, four-legged mammal with a narrow head, elongated neck, deep chest, gently curved back, and long legs. Body proportions, coat, and antlers vary by species and age.

## Body and Surface
The torso and legs are covered in short, dense fur. The coat is usually reddish-brown, chestnut, or gray-brown, with lighter fur on the belly and throat. Some species or young deer have pale spots. Fur follows the body's contours and has a soft, mostly matte appearance.

## Head and Features
The head tapers into a narrow muzzle. Large ears project from the sides of the head, and the eyes sit laterally for a wide field of view. The nose is dark and slightly moist. Antlers, when present, are hard, branching bone that is shed and regrown; they are not permanent horns.

## Legs and Feet
Long, slender limbs have defined joints and short fur. Each foot ends in a cloven hoof with two main hard, dark keratin digits. The short tail may reveal a pale underside, depending on the species.

## Modeling and Materials
Use natural brown color variation, fine directional fur detail, and matte coat roughness. Give the nose and eyes a subtle moist gloss, and use harder, less reflective materials for antlers and hooves. Match antler shape, markings, and proportions to the chosen species.
