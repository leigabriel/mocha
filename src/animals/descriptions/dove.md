# Dove

## Appearance
A small to medium-sized bird with a rounded body, relatively small head, short neck, compact beak, and tapered wings. The silhouette is soft and balanced, with proportions varying by species.

## Body and Surface
The body is covered in fine, overlapping feathers that create a smooth, softly layered appearance. Plumage commonly includes gray, beige, brown, white, or iridescent patches around the neck. Color and markings differ among wild and domestic varieties.

## Head and Features
The head is rounded, with small lateral eyes and a short beak. A soft cere may be visible at the base of the beak. The neck can show subtle iridescence, especially in some common urban and wild species.

## Wings, Tail, and Feet
The wings are broad and tapered, with layered flight feathers. The tail is usually medium-length and may fan during flight or display. Short legs end in three forward toes and one backward toe, with small claws.

## Modeling and Materials
Use fine feather layering and subtle tonal variation rather than a fur texture. Add restrained sheen to iridescent neck feathers where appropriate. Keep the beak and feet distinct from the plumage with smoother, slightly harder materials.
