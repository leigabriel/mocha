# Horse

## Appearance
A large, strong, four-legged herbivore with a long neck, deep chest, elongated head, muscular body, and long limbs. The back supports the saddle region, while the hindquarters provide much of the power for movement.

## Body and Surface
The body is covered in short, fine hair. Coat colors include bay, chestnut, black, gray, and many other patterns. The mane and tail contain longer hair, while the lower legs may have additional feathering in some breeds.

## Head and Features
The head is elongated with a broad forehead, large lateral eyes, mobile ears, wide nostrils, and a soft muzzle. The mane grows along the crest of the neck, and the forelock falls between the ears. The jaw is strong, with visible lips and large teeth when the mouth is open.

## Legs and Hooves
Long limbs have clearly defined joints and relatively little muscle below the knee and hock. Each leg ends in a single solid hoof made of keratin. The hooves are hard and curved, with a distinct hoof wall and sole.

## Modeling and Materials
Use species- and breed-appropriate proportions, coat color, mane, and tail. The coat should appear short and matte, while mane and tail hair need separate strand or card detail. Keep the muzzle slightly moist, the eyes reflective, and the hooves harder and less reflective than the coat.
