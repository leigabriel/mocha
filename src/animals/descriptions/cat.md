# Cat

## Appearance
A small, agile feline with a flexible spine, compact torso, rounded head, short muzzle, and four limbs. Body shape, face profile, coat length, and tail proportions vary by breed.

## Body and Surface
The body is covered in fur ranging from short and sleek to long and fluffy. Coat colors and patterns include solid colors, stripes, spots, patches, and color-point markings. Fur direction follows the torso and limbs, with longer hair possible around the neck, belly, tail, and ears.

## Head and Features
The face has forward-facing eyes, triangular or rounded ears, a small nose, and prominent whiskers. The pupils change shape with lighting. Whiskers emerge from distinct follicles around the muzzle, and the mouth contains small canine teeth.

## Legs, Paws, and Tail
The legs are slender and flexible, ending in padded paws with retractable claws. Most cats have five digits on each front paw, including a dewclaw, and four on each hind paw. The long tail aids balance and communicates mood.

## Modeling and Materials
Match body proportions and fur length to the intended breed. Use soft, directional fur detail, varied coat colors, and a slightly glossy eye and nose surface. Keep paw pads, claws, and whiskers distinct from the main fur material.
