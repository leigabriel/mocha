# Parrot

## Appearance
A compact to medium-sized bird with an upright posture, rounded chest, short back, strong curved beak, and a long or short tail depending on species. Parrots vary widely in size and silhouette.

## Body and Surface
The body is covered in overlapping feathers. Many species have vivid greens, reds, blues, yellows, or mixed patterns, while others have more muted colors. Feathers form layered surfaces rather than fur; their edges and direction follow the wings, body, and tail.

## Head and Features
The head is often large relative to the body. The thick hooked beak has a curved upper mandible and a shorter lower mandible. Eyes are round and glossy, and a bare or feathered skin ring may surround them. The tongue is muscular and helps manipulate food.

## Wings, Tail, and Feet
Broad wings contain layered flight feathers, while the tail may be long and tapered. Feet are zygodactyl: two toes point forward and two backward, with curved claws for gripping branches.

## Modeling and Materials
Create overlapping feather layers and clear flight-feather shapes. Use species-accurate color placement; avoid treating the entire body as one smooth surface. Feathers are generally low to moderate gloss, while the beak, eyes, and claws have harder, smoother surfaces.
