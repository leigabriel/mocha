# Ostrich

## Appearance
A very large, flightless bird with a tall upright body, long neck, small head, broad torso, and exceptionally long legs. The wings are present but are not used for powered flight.

## Body and Surface
The body is covered in loose, shaggy feathers that can appear soft and separated rather than tightly layered. Adult males commonly have black body plumage with white wing and tail feathers; females and juveniles are generally gray-brown. Coloration varies with sex and age.

## Head and Features
The head is small relative to the body, with large eyes and a short, flat beak. The long neck has sparse, fine feathering and may show exposed skin. The overall head-neck silhouette is narrow compared with the bulky torso.

## Legs and Feet
The long, powerful legs have visible joints and relatively little feather coverage. Each foot has two toes, including a larger inner toe with a strong claw. The wings are small relative to the body and have long display feathers.

## Modeling and Materials
Use loose feather geometry or layered feather cards for the body and wings, with finer coverage on the neck. Keep the legs visibly long and muscular, and model the two-toed feet accurately. Avoid giving the ostrich the three- or four-toed feet of other birds.
