# Dog

## Appearance
A domesticated canine with a body, head, leg length, and tail shape that vary greatly by breed. Common features include a muzzle, upright or floppy ears, four limbs, and a flexible tail.

## Body and Surface
The coat may be short, medium, long, curly, wiry, or double-layered. Colors and patterns include solid coats, patches, brindling, ticking, and saddle markings. Fur direction follows the body, with longer hair possible on the ears, chest, legs, and tail.

## Head and Features
The muzzle length and skull shape vary by breed. The nose is usually dark and moist, the eyes are forward-facing, and the ears may stand upright or fold down. The mouth contains prominent canine teeth and a flexible tongue.

## Legs, Paws, and Tail
Legs range from short and sturdy to long and slender. Paws have four main weight-bearing toes and claws, with a dewclaw sometimes present. Tail length, curl, and carriage vary by breed.

## Modeling and Materials
Choose a specific breed before defining proportions, ears, muzzle, coat, and tail. Use fur materials suited to the coat type and avoid a uniform texture across all breeds. Give the nose and eyes subtle gloss, and distinguish claws and paw pads from the coat.
